# Radiante — Roadmap

Where the project is heading after 1.1.0. Nothing here is scheduled; it is a direction, not a promise. Bugs and
ideas are tracked as [issues](../../issues); finished work is in the [changelog](changelog.md).

- **A cleaner picture.** Less noise in every pipeline (closer to Minecraft Bedrock), water and glass that look right,
  and a less plastic look.
- **Closer to Bedrock RTX.** Light, shadows and caustics that match it where it matters.
- **Faster on low-end RTX cards.** A preset that holds 60 fps with DLSS or FSR on a mid-range card.
- **Rock solid.** No crashes when settings change, no visual glitches that need a restart, and a visual smoke test run
  before every release.
- **Works with more.** AMD and Intel GPUs, the most used mods, Distant Horizons without blinking LODs, controllers.
- **More to play with.** Wind on leaves and grass, and other optional effects, off by default.
